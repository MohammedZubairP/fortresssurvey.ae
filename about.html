Introduction

Fortress Surveying Engineering Services L.L.C.

We deliver a comprehensive range of certified surveying and cartographic services to developers, contractors, consultants and government bodies. Our services are designed to support projects at every stage, from reconnaissance and control establishment through construction setting-out, verification and as-built surveys, complete with final reports and documentation.

Our team of licensed graduate engineers operates within the Gulf region, giving every client the benefit of local expertise, rapid mobilisation, and deep familiarity with Dubai government services, procedures and project requirements. This local focus allows us to respond efficiently to project needs, coordinate closely with site teams, and provide surveying support aligned with the practical requirements of construction and development projects across the Emirates.

At Fortress, we combine professional surveying expertise with modern equipment and established field practices to deliver dependable results within the required project timelines. Every assignment is approached with attention to detail, clear communication, and a strong understanding of the responsibilities involved in supporting active construction and development environments.

Our vision

Survey data as a foundation for better decisions.

We regard survey data as more than a project need. It is a solid foundation for better decisions. We want to be the surveying partner that consultants, contractors, developers and project teams can rely on at every stage, when timely, precise and understandable information is crucial. A good survey should offer more than just measurements; it should offer assurance, clarity, and a reliable source of information for the work that follows.

By combining seasoned survey experts, technology and a firm grasp of real building requirements, we strive to make every survey more beneficial, every output more dependable, and every project decision more assured.
